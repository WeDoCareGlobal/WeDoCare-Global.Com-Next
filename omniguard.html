The problem

AI agents are becoming operational

They can read data, call tools, spend money, and touch production systems.

Without explicit policy and inspectable evidence

Autonomy becomes operational risk.

Security reviews block deployment

Teams cannot expand agent access until actions are constrained, approvals required, and decisions explainable.

One control plane. Four enforcement layers.

How it works

  1. 1

    Register agent & tools

    Define agents, tools, data sources, and risk tiers in the policy.

  2. 2

    Define policy & approvals

    Set allow/block/review rules, rate limits, and human approvers.

  3. 3

    Route actions through OmniGuard

    Every agent request passes through the 6-layer inspection pipeline.

  4. 4

    Approve, block, or allow with evidence

    Decisions returned with governance headers and audit chain.

  5. 5

    Export evidence to SIEM / GRC

    Hash-linked audit records exported to Splunk, Elastic, Datadog, or webhook.
