FoundationCentar

Explore governance-first foundations for operational AI deployment and accountability.

Control question

How do you coordinate agents with explicit ownership and human review?

View orchestration demo View repository

FoundationCentar is a One-Click Agent Factory that orchestrates multi-agent workflows with human governance, bounded economics, and evidence-first verification. Every agent action flows through an explicit authority chain.

  • Human CEO → AI Lead → Chiefs → Workers — explicit authority hierarchy
  • Economic loop — mission → budget → execution → cost → evidence → verification
  • Evidence-first verification — no action is complete without human sign-off
  • Unified identity — Google → GitHub/ORCID/Zenodo OAuth flow
  • Local AI bridge — cloud control plane with local runtime execution

Authority hierarchy

Human CEO → AI Lead → Team Leads (Chiefs) → Workers → Verifier → Outcome → Learning

Every decision flows up the chain for review; every outcome flows back down for learning. No agent acts outside its bounded authority.

Core loops

How it works

One mission. One chain of command.

1

Mission defined

CEO sets the mission with budget and boundaries.

2

Chiefs plan

Team leads decompose the mission into worker tasks.

3

Workers execute

Agents execute within bounded authority and rate limits.

4

Verifier reviews

Human review gates every outcome before it is accepted.
