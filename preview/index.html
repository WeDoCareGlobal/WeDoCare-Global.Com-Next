The approach

Build the capability. Keep the control.

The useful question isn't only what an agent can do. It is how you know it did the right thing, under which policy, with which evidence.

01

Set the boundaries.

Make tool access, rate limits, credential scope, and human review explicit before expanding autonomy. A policy that is not written down is not a policy.

02

Connect the evidence.

Ground responses in knowledge, record every decision with a hash-linked trail, and keep the inspection layer in front of the systems you actually run.

03

Earn the next step.

Move from demonstration to deployment through tests, operational checks, and stated acceptance criteria. A passing test is evidence, not a certification.
